Paradise Run – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Paradise Run: Kids Learning is a learning game for young children, made by Metrizone ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What stays on the phone

The child's name, profile photo, voice recordings, mini-me character, settings and learning progress are stored only on the phone. We never receive them. The camera and microphone are used only when a parent chooses to, inside the parent area.

Anonymous play stats

To improve the game, the app sends anonymous usage counts: how many times the game is opened, how many levels are finished in each learning pack, the app version, and the date of first and last use. These are linked to a random anonymous ID created by the app, not to any person or device identifier. They contain no names, photos, voices or contact details. Parents can switch this off in Parent area > Stay in touch > Anonymous play stats.

Optional parent sign-in

In the parent area (behind a grown-ups-only question), a parent may choose to Sign in with Google to receive updates about new levels, new games and our videos. One sign-up is shared by all Metrizone games. If they do, we store the parent's name and email address and the date of sign-in. We use it only to send updates about our games and our channel. We never sell or share it, and never use it for ads.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), protected by Google's security and encrypted in transit.

Deleting your data

  • Parent details: in the app, go to Parent area > Stay in touch > Remove my details. This deletes your name, email and account immediately, for all Metrizone games. You can also email us at karthik.outlookapps@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we will delete them within 30 days.
  • Anonymous stats: switch them off in the app, or uninstall the app. To have past anonymous stats deleted, email us.
  • Everything on the phone: uninstall the app.

Children

The game itself never asks children for personal information. Sign-in is for parents only and is never required to play. There are no ads and no purchases in the child's area.
